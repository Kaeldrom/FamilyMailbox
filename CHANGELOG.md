# Changelog

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-14

- If Syndicator (part of Baganator) is installed, Family Mailbox imports what it has already recorded. That covers characters you haven't logged into since installing Family Mailbox, plus banks and mailboxes you haven't opened yet. Your own scans replace the imported data as soon as you open the bank or mailbox.
- The status line says when data came from Syndicator.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-14

- Fixed: on WoW Forever, characters have surnames instead of realms. 1.0.0 read each surname as a realm, so no characters showed in "Send to family" and mail between them wasn't tracked. Characters are now grouped by full name and ruleset, and existing data is moved over automatically.
- Mail is addressed to the full name ("Gromir Crypmaghar").
- New All / Bags / Bank / Mail / Worn filter buttons.
- Characters with unread mail show "mail waiting", even away from a mailbox.
