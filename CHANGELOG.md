# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- **Hand-me-downs** (new tab, or `/fm hand`): things one character holds that another could use.
  - Gear that isn't soulbound, that the other character's class wears (right armour type for their level, weapons they can use, sensible stats), and that beats what they have on now - or is ready within 3 levels.
  - Materials for their main professions (leather for a leatherworker, cloth for a tailor, dust for an enchanter...) when the holder has no use for them. Cooking, Fishing, First Aid and gathering professions don't count.
  - Click one, or "Post all", to put it on the post list; post it from whoever has it at a mailbox. Pick a character on the left to see just theirs.
  - A line in chat when you log in on a character holding hand-me-downs.
- Professions and soulbound items are now remembered for each character (log in on each once to fill them in).

## 1.3.0 - 2026-11-15

- **Post several items at once.** In the Items list, click items to pick them (a tick and gold highlight show what's picked), or Shift-click to pick a range. A bar appears at the bottom: **Post N to...** lets you choose a character, and every picked item goes on their post list in one go. **Pick all** picks everything in the current list (handy after a search), and **Clear** starts again. Picks are kept while you search, so you can pick herbs, search for ore and pick some of that too.
- Right-click still posts a single item, as before.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-14

- If Syndicator (part of Baganator) is installed, Family Mailbox imports what it has already recorded. That covers characters you haven't logged into since installing Family Mailbox, plus banks and mailboxes you haven't opened yet. Your own scans replace the imported data as soon as you open the bank or mailbox.
- The status line says when data came from Syndicator.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-14

- Fixed: on WoW Forever, characters have surnames instead of realms. 1.0.0 read each surname as a realm, so no characters showed in "Send to family" and mail between them wasn't tracked. Characters are now grouped by full name and ruleset, and existing data is moved over automatically.
- Mail is addressed to the full name ("Gromir Crypmaghar").
- New All / Bags / Bank / Mail / Worn filter buttons.
- Characters with unread mail show "mail waiting", even away from a mailbox.
