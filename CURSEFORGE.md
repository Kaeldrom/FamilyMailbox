# Family Mailbox

**See what every character on your account is carrying, and post things between them** - made for accounts shared by a family, and just as handy for anyone with alts. For World of Warcraft Forever.

## One window for everyone
Type **/fm** (or use the addon button by the minimap). Pick a character, or "Everyone", and search.
- **All / Bags / Bank / Mail / Worn** - look in one place, or everywhere at once.
- Every item shows **who has it and where**.
- **Gold** for each character, plus the family total - including gold waiting in the mailbox.
- **Tooltips** on any item: "Family has 23", and which characters hold it.

## Post things between characters
- **Right-click an item and tick who it's for** - or pick several items and send them all to one character.
- At a mailbox, the **Send to family** panel addresses the letter and attaches everything on the list for that character in one click. You still press Send yourself.
- **Mail in transit:** things you post to a family character show in their mail straight away, before they log in.
- Soulbound items can't be posted - they're skipped and you're told.

## Hand-me-downs (new in 1.4.0)
The **Hand-me-downs** tab (or **/fm hand**) lists what your characters could pass to each other:
- **Gear** that isn't soulbound, suits the other character's class (armour type for their level, weapons they can use, stats that fit) and beats what they're wearing - or will be usable within a few levels.
- **Materials for their professions** - Gromir has Light Leather and Aldric is a leatherworker, so it's suggested for Aldric. Cooking, Fishing, First Aid and gathering don't count, and nothing is suggested if the holder uses it themselves.
- Click a suggestion, or **Post all**, to put it on the post list, then post it from whoever has it.
- A line in chat when you log in on a character holding hand-me-downs.

## Works with Syndicator / Baganator
If you use Baganator or Syndicator, Family Mailbox imports what they've already recorded, so every character and bank shows up straight away.

## Getting started
Log in once on each character, and open the bank and mailbox once, so Family Mailbox can record them.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| /fm | Open or close the window |
| /fm hand | Hand-me-downs |
| /fm tooltip | Family counts on tooltips on/off |
| /fm mailpanel | The mailbox panel on/off |
| /fm forget Name | Forget a deleted or transferred character |

Everything is saved on your computer only (per account) and never sent anywhere.
